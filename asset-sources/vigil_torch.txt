Vigil Torch texture source

Author: Codex, created for Chaze-2025/The-balance-of-good-and-evil, Phase 1.
License: All rights reserved, matching the repository mod license.
Source: Original pixel art drawn directly from the palette and matrix below.
No third-party pixels or Minecraft textures were copied or transformed.
The model JSON refers to built-in Minecraft template_torch/template_torch_wall;
those model files and Minecraft textures are not redistributed.

Export: 16x16 PNG, 8-bit RGBA, unpremultiplied alpha, no animation or metadata.
Transparent background; nearest-neighbor previews only. The center two-pixel
shaft aligns with the standard torch template UVs. Flame/gold collar and the
amethyst neck are deliberately small. In-game UV/appearance review is pending.
